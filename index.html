Controls
• Drag from your star to route 50% of orbiting defenders.

• Double-Click a star to trigger upgrade suction.

• Orbiters will spiral into the center. Upon reaching upgrade cost, remaining units are released back to orbit automatically!

• Defenders: Orbiters actively intercept incoming attack waves near their star.
